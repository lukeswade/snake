Snake Surge is a free, offline, single-player arcade snake game. Rated 4+.

NO ACCOUNTS / NO PURCHASES / NO PERMISSIONS
No sign-in, registration or account deletion — nothing is gated. No in-app
purchases, subscriptions or ads. The app requests no permissions at all: no
location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, notifications or App
Tracking Transparency. No IDFA, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.

WHAT IT IS
Classic snake with two twists: MERGE (three matching body segments fuse into
one higher-tier segment) and CHRONO-SURGE (crash with 50%+ surge banked and
you may spend it to rewind a few seconds and continue instead of dying).
Modes: Merge & Surge, Classic Survival, and a 60-second PvP Blitz against two
computer-controlled snakes. Audience: general, all ages.

HOW TO TEST (no credentials or sample files needed)
- Tap PLAY NOW; a 3-2-1 countdown begins the run.
- Steer: tap either side of the snake's path, or swipe. Keyboard: WASD/arrows.
- SURGE: at 100% meter tap the lightning button (bottom-right) or press Space.
- CHRONO-SURGE: crash with 50%+ surge; tap REVERSE TIME at the prompt.
- Menu (hamburger, top-right) holds modes, difficulty, skins, volume and the
  leaderboard. The book icon opens the full in-app rules.
- The whole game is bundled in the binary; please feel free to test in
  airplane mode.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None required to play. The only network call is an optional global high-score
leaderboard on our own Cloudflare infrastructure (Pages Functions + Workers
KV), first-party and developer-operated. A submission sends exactly: the
display name the player types, the score, and the date. No identifiers, no
account, no personal data. No auth providers, payment processors or AI
services are used.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
The app's only UGC is that optional 16-character leaderboard display name.
- Restricted to letters, digits, spaces and _ - . at 16 characters.
- A server-side blocklist rejects profanity, slurs, hate terms and self-harm
  references at submission, so objectionable names are never stored or shown.
  It normalises case, separators and leetspeak to catch evasion.
- The leaderboard screen has a "Report a name" link (emails the developer).
- Any entry is removed on request; stated in the privacy policy at
  https://snake.lukewade.net/privacy
- There is no user-to-user interaction anywhere in the app — no messaging,
  chat, profiles, follows or comments — so there is no user to block.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical in all regions. No geolocation, no region-gated content, free
everywhere, English-only, one shared worldwide leaderboard.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. All content is original and first-party, including
procedurally synthesised audio (Web Audio API — no audio files). Bundled
fonts are SIL Open Font License; the Lucide icon set is MIT.

TESTED ON

Simulator (iPadOS 26.5). Portrait and landscape, and offline in airplane mode.
